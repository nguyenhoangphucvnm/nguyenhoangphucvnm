# hey, I'm Phuc 👋

I build products — from **idea to implementation to shipping**.

My background is in **mobile and web development**, with a strong focus on **architecture, and developer experience**. But technology is only part of the job.

I care about **building what matters**, understanding the problem behind the feature, keeping products simple, and shipping software that people can actually use.

Based in Vietnam 🇻🇳

---

### what I care about

**Product thinking**  
Understanding the problem before jumping into implementation.

**Building**  
Turning ideas and requirements into simple, reliable software.

**Shipping**  
Moving from "almost done" to something real, usable, and delivered.

**Engineering**  
Good architecture, maintainable code, practical trade-offs, and systems that can evolve.

**Developer experience**  
Tools, workflows, monorepos, automation, and patterns that make teams faster without making things unnecessarily complicated.
